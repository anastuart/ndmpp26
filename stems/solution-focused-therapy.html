Question Stems

Solution Focused Therapy

Stems for working with what's already going right — pre-session change, exceptions, scaling, the miracle question, and coping. The aim is not to solve the problem but to help the client notice and amplify what's already moving them toward their preferred future.

Opening the Conversation
Set the frame early: this is a conversation about what the client wants, not about cataloguing what's wrong. Curiosity, not assessment.
01What needs to come out of our time together for you to feel it was worthwhile?
02How will you know that coming here today was useful?
03What's the most important thing for me to understand about you?
04What do you do when you're not [struggling with the problem] — what does the rest of your life look like?
05If we use this hour well, what will be different by the time you walk out?

Pre-Session Change
Change often begins the moment a person decides to seek help. Asking about it early signals that you expect movement and helps the client notice it.
01Between making this appointment and coming in today — has anything been even a little better?
02What have you noticed yourself doing differently since you decided to come?
03How did you manage to take that step?
04What does it tell you about yourself, that you got here today?
05What would you like to keep doing more of, from what's already been working?

Best Hopes & Preferred Future
Anchor the work in what the client wants — not what they want to get rid of. Keep the language concrete, observable, and in the client's own words.
01What are your best hopes from talking together?
02If those hopes were realised, what would be different in your day-to-day life?
03What would you be doing instead of [the problem]?
04When this is no longer a problem for you — what will be there in its place?
05What would tell you we don't need to meet anymore?
06What kind of person do you want to be on the other side of this?
07What's the smallest sign that things are heading in the right direction?

The Miracle Question
A staple SFT move. Slow it down — the power is in the detail. Stay with the small, observable changes the client describes, and follow them with "and what else?"
01Suppose tonight, while you're sleeping, a miracle happens — and the problem that brought you here is solved. But because you're asleep, you don't know it has happened. When you wake up tomorrow, what's the first thing you'd notice that tells you the miracle has occurred?
02What's the very first small sign you'd notice?
03What would you be doing differently that morning?
04Who would be the first person to notice — and what would they see?
05How would they respond to you differently? And how would that be for you?
06What else would be different? And what else?
07When did you last have a small piece of this miracle already happening — even briefly?

Exception-Finding
The problem is never happening all the time. Exceptions are the cracks where solutions already live — find them, get curious about them, and credit the client for them.
01When is the problem not happening — or happening less?
02Tell me about a recent time things went a bit better — even slightly.
03What was different about that time?
04What were you doing differently?
05How did you make that happen?
06What would it take to have more moments like that?
07Who else noticed it was a better day? What did they pick up on?
08When have you faced something like this before and come through it?

Scaling Questions
A 0-to-10 scale gives the client a way to measure their own progress, confidence, and motivation. The interesting work is always in why the number isn't lower — and what one point higher would look like.
01On a scale of 0 to 10, where 10 is the day after the miracle and 0 is the worst things have ever been — where are you today?
02How come you're at a [4] and not a [1]? What's already in place?
03What would a [5] look like — what would you notice yourself doing?
04What's the smallest thing that would move you up by half a point?
05On a scale of 0 to 10, how confident are you that you can take that next step?
06On a scale of 0 to 10, how willing are you to do something about this right now?
07If you were at a [7], what would your day look like that's not happening yet?
08Where on the scale would be good enough — you don't need to be at 10?

Coping Questions
For clients in crisis, despair, or who insist nothing is working. Coping questions honour the difficulty without minimising it, while drawing attention to the strength it takes simply to keep going.
01Given how hard things have been — how have you managed to keep going?
02How come things aren't even worse?
03What's stopping this from sliding further?
04How did you get out of bed this morning, with all that's going on?
05What's helped you survive what you've already been through?
06Where do you find the strength to keep showing up?
07What's the smallest thing you've done this week that took some effort?

Resources & Strengths
SFT assumes the client already has what they need — your job is to help them see it. Look for skills, supports, past successes, and qualities they may take for granted.
01What strengths have helped you face hard things before?
02What would the people who know you best say you're good at?
03When you've solved problems in the past, how did you do it?
04Who's in your corner — even one person?
05What helps you, even a little, on the harder days?
06If a friend were facing what you're facing — what would you tell them they had going for them?
07What's something you've done in the past that you're quietly proud of?

Relationship Questions
Bring others into the room. Asking what the client's partner, child, friend, or colleague would notice often surfaces specifics the client wouldn't volunteer about themselves.
01If your [partner / mum / best friend] were sitting here, what would they say is different about you on a good day?
02Who would be the first to notice things were getting better — and what would they pick up on?
03What would your [child] say they want for you?
04If I asked your [boss / GP] what's going better, what do you think they'd say?
05What difference would it make to the people around you, if even a little of this shifted?
06Who in your life would be least surprised that you came here today?

Compliments & Affirmations
SFT compliments are evidence-based — they reflect something the client has actually said or done. They're not flattery; they're observations that build the client's view of themselves as capable.
01I'm struck by how [specific quality] you've been, given everything.
02It takes something to keep showing up the way you have.
03How did you know to do [that specific helpful thing]?
04A lot of people in your shoes wouldn't have managed that — how did you?
05That's a really clear-eyed way of seeing it — where does that come from in you?
06You've done more than you're giving yourself credit for.

Closing & Between-Session Tasks
End by inviting the client to notice — not to fix. Tasks are observational, not prescriptive: what's working, what they want more of, what they're already doing.
01Between now and next time, notice what's happening in your life that you'd like to keep happening.
02Pay attention to the times the problem is smaller — and what you're doing in those moments.
03Pick one small thing from today's conversation and try doing more of it this week.
04Watch for the small signs that you're moving up the scale — even by half a point.
05What will you take away from today that feels useful?
06What's one thing you want to remind yourself of this week?

Follow-Up Sessions — "What's Better?"
Open every subsequent session with a presupposition of progress. "What's better?" sets a different frame from "How have you been?" — and pulls the client's attention toward signal, not noise.
01What's better since we last met — even a little?
02What did you do that helped that happen?
03How did you make that decision in the moment?
04What does that tell you about yourself?
05Where are you on the scale today, compared to last time?
06What needs to keep happening for that progress to stick?
07If there's been a setback — how did you stop it from being worse?
08What's the next small step from here?
