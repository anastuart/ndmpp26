Client Worksheets

Traffic Light Signals

A relapse-prevention tool for the ending phase of therapy — mapping the signals that tell the client where they are (green, yellow, red) and the actions that help in each zone. Useful as a portable summary the client can keep using after termination.

My Traffic Light Plan

To be completed together with a clinician — usually during the termination phase.

Use this worksheet to map out three zones — when I feel good, when I'm starting to feel less well, and when I'm not well. For each zone, note the signals (what I notice in myself, and what others might notice in me) and the actions (what I can do, and which strategies are most helpful).
