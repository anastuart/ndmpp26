My Mental Health Maintenance Plan

To be completed together with a clinician during the termination phase.

Much like your body needs a balanced diet and exercise to stay well, your mental health needs ongoing attention. Maintaining wellness involves practising self-care, using coping strategies, and knowing when to seek help. This plan summarises what works for you and what to watch out for, so you can keep using what you've learned long after therapy ends.
